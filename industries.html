Industries served

Mills, plants and workshops across Tamil Nadu rely on us for bearings that match their machines.

Textile

Spinning & weaving machinery bearings

Ring frames, looms, carding and spindle drives run for long hours at high speed. Low vibration and long grease life matter most.

What we usually supply

  • Deep groove ball
  • Angular contact
  • Needle roller
  • Belts and pulleys
See textile bearings

Paper

High-speed roller & cylindrical bearings

Press and dryer sections combine heat, moisture and heavy rolls. Roller bearings with good clearance and proper sealing keep them turning.

What we usually supply

  • Spherical roller
  • Cylindrical roller
  • Plummer blocks
See paper bearings

Steel

Heavy-duty mill & furnace bearings

Rolling mills, furnace fans and cooling beds see shock loads, scale and high temperature. Heavy-duty roller bearings are the standard answer.

What we usually supply

  • Tapered roller
  • Spherical roller
  • Cylindrical roller
See steel bearings

Cement

Crusher & kiln bearing solutions

Crushers, kiln support rollers, fans and mills work in dust with heavy, uneven loads. Self-aligning bearings with strong sealing last longer here.

What we usually supply

  • Spherical roller
  • Plummer blocks
  • Adapter sleeves
See cement bearings

Food Processing

Hygienic stainless steel bearings

Wash-down, steam and food-safe lubrication call for sealed, corrosion-resistant bearings that stay clean.

What we usually supply

  • Sealed ball bearings
  • Stainless housed units
  • Food-grade grease
See food processing bearings

Packaging

Conveyor & automation bearings

Fast start-stop cycles on conveyors, cartoners and fillers need smooth, sealed bearings and accurate drives.

What we usually supply

  • Sealed ball bearings
  • Pillow blocks
  • Timing belts
  • Chains
See packaging bearings

Agriculture

Farm equipment & harvester parts

Tractors, harvesters, pumps and threshers face mud, dust and vibration. Sealed and housed bearings cut down field breakdowns.

What we usually supply

  • Housed units
  • Tapered roller
  • Chains
See agriculture bearings

Wheel hubs & transmission bearings

Application guide

Typical starting points. Your machine, load and speed decide the final choice.

ApplicationUsual bearing choiceWhat matters
Ring frame spindles, loomsDeep groove ball, angular contactHigh speed with low vibration
Paper machine press and dryer rollsSpherical roller, cylindrical rollerHeavy rolls, heat, misalignment
Rolling mill and furnace fansTapered roller, spherical rollerShock load and high temperature
Crushers, kiln and mill drivesSpherical roller on plummer blocksDust, heavy and uneven load
Conveyor and packaging linesSealed ball bearings, pillow blocksLong running hours, easy fitting
Wheel hubs and gearboxesTapered roller, ball and needleCombined loads, tight tolerances
