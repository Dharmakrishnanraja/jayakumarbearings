About Jayakumar Bearings

A bearing and power-transmission dealer in Rajapalayam, serving mills, workshops and factories across Tamil Nadu for more than 35 years.

Genuine bearings, honest advice, ready stock

Jayakumar Bearings is an authorised dealer of SKF, TIMKEN, NSK and NBC. For more than 35 years we have supplied bearings and power-transmission products to industries in and around Rajapalayam, and across Tamil Nadu.

We keep over 1000 products on the shelf, from small ball bearings to heavy spherical rollers, so most stock orders leave the same day. If a size is not on the shelf, we tell you straight away and give you the real lead time.

A bearing that fails early usually fails because the wrong type, clearance or seal was chosen. That is why our team asks about the machine, the load and the speed before it quotes. Right bearing first time means fewer breakdowns for you.

Founder’s Desk

Built on trade. Led by service.

Rtn. P. A. Sivakumar Raja is the Founder and CEO of Jayakumar Bearings. He runs the business hands-on, from sourcing and supplier terms to supporting the spinning mills, stone crushers and windmill operators who depend on us across Tamil Nadu.

Beyond the shop, he has given more than 15 years to Rotary International, District 3212, as Secretary, Treasurer, President and now Chief Assistant Governor. His term as Club President brought “The Best Club” and “The Best President” awards; as Secretary he was named “The Best District Secretary”.

He serves on the boards of Rajapalayam Rajus’ College and Sri Kshatriya Vidhyalaya, works to bring better schooling to the community, and supports students’ studies and families’ essential needs through a personal trust. A devout and generous man, he is respected across Rajapalayam for it.

How an order works

From first message to the parcel leaving our shop.

  1. 1

    Tell us what you need

    Send a bearing number, the size, or the machine it goes into. A photo of the old bearing works too.

  2. 2

    We check the application

    We ask about load, speed, temperature and mounting, and match the type, clearance and seal.

  3. 3

    You get a clear quote

    Brand options with price, GST and availability, so you can choose what suits the job and the budget.

  4. 4

    We dispatch

    Stock items go out the same day. Anything we need to source, we confirm with a lead time first.

What you can count on

Four promises we keep on every order.

Genuine only

We buy from the manufacturers' channels and sell original, packed bearings. Not look-alikes.

Advice before the sale

Our team talks through the application. If a cheaper bearing will do the job, we say so.

Stock that is ready

A deep shelf of common sizes, so a breakdown does not become a long wait.

Straight pricing

Price, discount and GST shown clearly. The Bearing Price Finder lets you check yourself.

Brands we stand behind
