How to be sure your bearing is genuine

  • Buy from an authorised dealer

    Authorised dealers buy through the manufacturer's own channel, so the bearing, the box and the paperwork all trace back to the source.

  • Read the markings

    Genuine bearings carry a crisp brand name and part number on the ring, and the number matches the box.

  • Check the packing

    Look for clean printing, proper seals and consistent labels. Poor print and loose fit are warning signs.

  • Ask for the invoice

    A proper GST invoice from a known dealer is your proof of purchase and your route for any warranty query.
